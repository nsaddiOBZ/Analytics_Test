Connect to your Data Cube

The dashboards query the Obzervr - Data Cube semantic model through the Power BI REST API with your Microsoft identity. Power BI checks your access and applies row-level security, so you only see what you already see in Power BI.

Use your work account. You'll come straight back here, and the token renews on its own while you work.
Use an access token instead
Drop credentials.txt here, or click to choose it. Reads ACCESS_TOKEN=, WORKSPACE_ID= and DATASET_ID=.
Run az account get-access-token --resource https://analysis.windows.net/powerbi/api --query accessToken -o tsv and paste the result. Tokens last about an hour. In PowerShell: Get-PowerBIAccessToken -AsString.

AI mode settings

In AI mode a language model reads your question and the Data Cube's own list of tables and measures, and writes the DAX. Power BI still runs the query with your token and row-level security. The model sees your question, the cube's structure and the rows of each answer, never your Power BI token.

Gemini keys come from Google AI Studio, Claude keys from the Claude Console. The key goes only to that provider, straight from this browser.
